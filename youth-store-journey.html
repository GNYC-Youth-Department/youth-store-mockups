The Journey

Grow·Serve·Belong

Every patch, pin, and uniform marks a step in a young person’s walk with God. The store makes that journey visible, shared, and celebrated — and this year, we walk it with intention.

“Be very careful, then, how you live — making the most of every opportunity.” — Ephesians 5:15-16
The idea

Not just merchandise — markers of a journey

The store becomes the place where growth in Christ is made tangible. Three pillars carry it, each anchored in Scripture.

🌱

Grow

“Grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord.” — 2 Peter 3:18
In the store: honors, ranks, and class progression — the visible signs of God-given gifts being developed.
🤝

Serve

“Serve one another humbly in love.” — Galatians 5:13
In the store: mission-trip support, Community in Action, service awards — gifts put to work for others.
👪

Belong

“Spur one another on toward love and good deeds.” — Hebrews 10:24
In the store: uniforms, neckerchiefs, club identity — the marks of belonging to a family on mission.
✨
A healthy frame, on purpose. Recognition here is encouragement and celebration of faithfulness — spurring one another on (Heb 10:24) — never status or boasting. We lift up growth and service, not self.

The trail

Missions & Milestones

Outdoor brands build a culture around the trail — ours is real, and eternal. Youth earn milestones by walking the journey, redeemed for gifts that point back to the mission — never vanity, always growth.

🌱 Grow
Complete an honor+50
Reach a new class / rank+100
Finish a Bible-reading plan+30
🤝 Serve
Join a service project+40
Go on a mission trip+200

👪 Belong

Bring a friend along+25

We steward the gifts God gives (Matthew 25) and spur one another on (Hebrews 10:24). “Well done, good and faithful servant.” — Matthew 25:21
